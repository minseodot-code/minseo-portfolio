Hankyung.com Marketing Job Project · Apr–Jun 2026

Building a character-led Instagram account for Midoin

minseo.chung Midoin runs 32+ restaurants, and its official Instagram (about 11K followers) mostly shared menus and promotions. That left room for a second account built on character and storytelling.

Our audience: women in their 20s and 30s (core 22–29) who find restaurants on Reels and Explore, skip anything that looks like an ad, and save and send what feels like their own story. So we created Midori, a picky eater who speaks for them: "a meal worth recording, for picky tastes."

#competitoranalysis #SWOT #personas #AISAS #ownedearnedpaid

My role: Team leader of three. Ran the eight-week timeline and assigned roles, produced Reels and four-panel comics (ChatGPT, Kling AI, CapCut, Canva), and designed the Meta Ads A/B test.

An English guide for students arriving from six countries

minseo.chung A four-day international career program for high school students from Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand and more. They read the guide in their second language, on their phones, in an unfamiliar city, so it kept sections short, put the rules first, and gave each venue a bit of local history.

My role: Worked on the program from planning to on-site operations. Built the English participant guide in Notion, translated about ten showcase team and game descriptions between Korean and English, and co-planned cultural missions at Jongmyo and Ikseon-dong. I also translated the 20-page 2027 COYC guidelines from JA Asia Pacific into Korean.

Aix-en-Provence, France · Aug 2024 – Jun 2025

Introducing Korea in French, at a dinner table

K-beauty in class. I gave a K-beauty presentation in French with live product demos, a quiz and giveaways. Several classmates later bought the products. I also visited K-EXPO France 2024.

International Dinner. I invited friends from different countries, chose mild dishes like japchae and bulgogi for those new to spicy food, and asked everyone to bring a dish from home. Before long, friends were asking "What time tonight?" before I had even sent the invite.
